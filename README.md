# MBAKU Online Store: concept website

*Quality products. Affordable prices.*

A one-page online store for **MBAKU Online Store** in Tunapuna, Trinidad & Tobago. It sells car stereos, smart-home gear, power tools, audio and gadgets, with pickup or delivery nationwide.

Concept build by **Portside Digital**. Product photos come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) (Unsplash License). Prices, stock levels and reviews are samples.

## Shop
- 14 products in 5 categories, with live search (press `/` anywhere; "under 300" works too), category chips with counts, and sorting
- Discount stickers, "only X left" stock bars and a quick view with specs and quantity
- A cart drawer with a fly-to-cart animation and a free-delivery meter. The cart is kept after a reload
- **Order on WhatsApp:** the cart writes the full order (items, delivery area and total) into a WhatsApp message to 793-2163
- A delivery map of Trinidad & Tobago: pick an area and the van drives the route, then shows the fee and arrival time ($40 in Trinidad, $60 to Tobago, free pickup)
- This week's drop, with a live countdown, a claimed-stock bar and a bundle price

## Scroll craft
GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis. Reduced motion is respected.
- A loader where a tape strip slices the MBAKU wordmark open
- A rotating sunburst hero (from the store's logo) with product cards that follow the cursor
- Tilted marquees that speed up with the scroll
- **The unboxing:** a pinned CSS-3D box turns, its flaps open, and products pop out while the quality-check promise reads in
- **The price slash:** the hardware price gets struck through, the MBAKU price counts down to $499, and a "you save" stamp lands
- A horizontal best-sellers rail, flyer-style with spec chips
- A pinned WhatsApp chat that plays out the order flow step by step
- Footer mantra words that slide together as you arrive

No orders are placed by this demo. The WhatsApp button opens a pre-filled chat.
